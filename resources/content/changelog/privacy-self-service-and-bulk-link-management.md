We've shipped major link management and privacy features that give users complete control over their link catalog, security parameters, and personal data.

Highlights:
- **Self-Service Privacy Portal**: Export complete account data in standardized machine-readable JSON/CSV archives, download historical logs, and schedule irreversible automated deletion with a clear grace period.
- **Password-Protected Short Links**: Gate sensitive destinations behind an edge-rendered password prompt with rate-limited, brute-force-resistant attempt throttling.
- **Device & Geolocation Targeting Editor**: Dynamic routing rules based on viewer operating system (iOS, Android, macOS, Windows, Linux) and country code, managed via an ID-preserving visual editor.
- **Bulk Table Operations & CSV Import**: Bulk activate, pause, or tag dozens of links at once directly in the dashboard, plus a multi-column CSV importer for large link catalog migrations.
- **Public Domain Selector**: Added an interactive selector allowing users on public shortener surfaces to choose their destination domain (href.nz, href.yt) before shortening.
