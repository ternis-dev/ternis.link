We've launched **qr.href.nz**, a dedicated programmable QR code studio and developer API designed to replace ad-supported, low-resolution QR generators with a fast, privacy-preserving vector instrument.

Highlights:
- **Vector SVG & PNG Generation**: High-resolution, mathematically crisp QR code generation with configurable error correction levels (L, M, Q, H) and custom pixel density.
- **Multi-Type Payload Routing**: Built-in formatters for URLs, Wi-Fi networks (WPA2/WPA3), vCard 4.0 digital business cards, WhatsApp click-to-chat links, SMS messages, Ethereum/Bitcoin addresses, and geo coordinates.
- **Developer REST API**: Direct programmatic QR rendering via `/v1/qr` endpoints with immediate binary or base64 responses, ideal for inclusion in invoices, packaging labels, and dynamic event badges.
- **Generation Telemetry & Counter**: Live network-wide aggregate generation counters without logging sensitive payload content or client IP addresses.
- **Dedicated Landing Page**: Interactive generator workspace with live SVG preview, payload type switcher, color picker, and one-click downloads.
