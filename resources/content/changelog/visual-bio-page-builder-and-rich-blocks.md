Our link-in-bio engine received a complete overhaul with the release of the **Visual Bio Page-Builder** and a rich suite of interactive content blocks.

Highlights:
- **Visual Drag-and-Drop Builder**: Real-time live phone preview canvas with instantaneous reordering, sub-page navigation, and button pause/schedule toggles.
- **RSVP & Event Blocks**: Collect event registrations and display live headcount meters directly on your profile page with automated confirmation badges.
- **Location, Map, and Audio Blocks**: Embed interactive Google/Apple Maps location pins, streaming audio player tracks, and downloadable asset cards.
- **Quote & Discount Coupon Blocks**: Present promotional coupons with one-click copy and styled pull-quotes for creator testimonials.
- **Countdowns & Announcement Bars**: Timed announcement banners for product launches, livestreams, and ticket sales with automatic expiration redirects.
- **Branding & Theme Customization**: Automatic light/dark system theme matching, customizable button corner styles, social icon rows, downloadable vCards, and custom favicons.
