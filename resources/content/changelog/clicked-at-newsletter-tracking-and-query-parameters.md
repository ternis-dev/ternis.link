Email creators and newsletter publishers now have a dedicated home at **clicked.at**, tailored specifically for measuring email engagement without invasive tracking pixels or third-party cookies.

Highlights:
- **clicked.at Landing Page**: Dedicated electric indigo marketing landing page featuring live click simulation, 3-step walkthrough, locale switcher, and Schema.org FAQPage structured data.
- **Dynamic Campaign Tags & Query Inspection**: Clicks now capture query parameters in structured JSON alongside dynamic tagging, giving publishers granular insight into newsletter issues, link positions, and subscriber segments.
- **Privacy-Preserving Visitor Attribution**: Daily unique visitors are measured using salted, rotating cryptographic hashes that prevent cross-site or cross-day surveillance while ensuring accurate unique click counts.
- **Campaign UTM Tagging at Redirect**: Short links can append or pass through campaign UTM parameters (`utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`, `utm_content`) to destination analytics platforms automatically.
- **Admin Statistics & Telemetry**: Comprehensive administrator overview tracking aggregate click volume, system health, and domain distribution across the network.
