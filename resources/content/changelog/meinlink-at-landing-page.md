The network gains a German home: **meinlink.at** gets its own landing page and shortener UI, separate from the href.nz sketchbook — an Amt-style theme (Inter grotesk, BEWILLIGT stamp) with a fully German form, errors, and FAQ.

Same fair rules as href.nz underneath: guest shortening with quotas, auto-generated slugs, and bot protection. On top of that, meinlink.at offers a **domain toggle** (create on `meinlink.at` or `href.nz`), a **customizable slug length** (5 to 9 characters), and an **expiration date** (up to 365 days out) — plus duplicate detection, a quota meter, and QR codes for every link.

Like every public page it ships with full SEO metadata, JSON-LD structured data (including an FAQ section), and a Markdown-friendly shape for agents. Rollout note: the landing page and UI landed today; the `meinlink.at` domain itself is registered on September 29th, 2026.
