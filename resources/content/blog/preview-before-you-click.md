Short links ask for trust: the whole point is hiding a long URL behind a short one. The preview page on href.nz returns the favor — open `href.nz/preview/{anything}` and see the destination without going there. No redirect fires, no click is counted, no tracking row is written. You decide with full information.

## What the preview shows

Paste a slug or any direct URL and the sandbox lays out where it points, including a verdict from the same scanner-junk detector that guards link creation. A link to a login page you don't recognize, or a destination shaped like a vulnerability-probe payload, shows up as exactly that — before your browser ever touches it.

This makes preview the right habit for links from strangers: chat messages, forum posts, QR codes on lampposts. If the destination looks wrong, close the tab; nothing executed, nothing counted.

## For link creators, a second audience

Preview isn't only defensive. Send a preview link alongside the short one in support threads and cautious communities — it signals you have nothing to hide and measurably lifts click-through where trust is thin. The people most careful about what they click are often the people most worth reaching.
