Not every link deserves an account. For the quick share — a chat message, a forum post, a QR on a cardboard sign — [href.nz](https://href.nz) shortens links with nothing but a destination URL: no signup, no email, no password.

## How it works

Paste the long URL, solve the bot check, get a short link. Slugs are auto-generated (8 characters) because choosing names is a member perk; the link resolves exactly like any other, with the same fast cached redirects. Turn it into a QR code in one more step, straight from the result.

Fair use keeps it abuse-free: throttling plus **50 links a day per address**, counted by hash so no raw addresses are ever stored. Rejected scanner-junk destinations don't burn your quota — only links that actually get created count.

## When to sign in instead

Guests get the essentials; members get control. Signing in (free, via Ternis Auth, still no passwords) unlocks custom slugs, shorter codes, QR downloads, click analytics, expiry dates, tags, custom domains, and the API — plus higher daily limits. The quota meter on the guest form tells you exactly where you stand, and when you keep hitting the ceiling, that is the nudge: your links want a home.
