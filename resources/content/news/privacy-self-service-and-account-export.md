Privacy isn't a marketing claim; it's an engineering commitment. Today we're shipping a comprehensive **Privacy Self-Service Portal** inside account settings.

Shortener platforms frequently make account deletion difficult or retain click telemetry indefinitely. In contrast, our self-service tools provide immediate, autonomous data ownership:

- **Complete Data Archive Export**: Request and download a consolidated ZIP archive containing your full link catalog, click telemetry summaries, targeting rules, and bio page configurations in open JSON and CSV formats.
- **Scheduled Automated Deletion**: Schedule permanent account deletion with a configurable grace period. When the grace period concludes, your account record, API keys, personal metadata, and private link definitions are permanently scrubbed from production databases and edge caches.
- **Non-Invasive Aggregate Telemetry**: Historical public aggregate counts remain safely preserved in anonymous counters without associating personal identities or network traces.

To explore these features, navigate to **Account Settings → Privacy & Security**.
