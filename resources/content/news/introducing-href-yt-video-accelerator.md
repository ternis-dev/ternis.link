We're excited to announce **[href.yt](https://href.yt/)**, our new specialized video and creator link accelerator designed for YouTubers, streamers, podcasters, and digital storytellers.

When sharing video clips, every second counts. Traditional URL shorteners often introduce intermediate redirect delays, strip timestamp query parameters, or worse—subject viewers to intrusive ad-walls and CAPTCHAs that kill watch time before the video even starts.

**href.yt changes the equation:**
- **Timestamp Preservation**: Append `?t=1m42s` or `?t=90s` to any destination, and href.yt passes it directly through to the player so viewers land on the exact moment you want them to see.
- **Optimized for Live Chat & Overlays**: Clean 7-character vanity and randomized slugs fit neatly into Twitch Nightbot/StreamElements commands, Discord announcements, and OBS broadcast overlays.
- **Direct Edge Routing**: Resolves via TLS 1.3 with sub-10ms response times and zero intermediary ad barriers.
- **Creator Studio Accounts**: Log in with Ternis Auth SSO to claim custom vanity slugs (like `href.yt/live` or `href.yt/highlights`), generate scoped API keys, and track real-time click volume.
