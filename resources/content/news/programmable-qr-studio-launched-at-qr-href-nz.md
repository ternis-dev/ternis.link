Physical media deserves clean, reliable digital entry points. Today we're launching **[qr.href.nz](https://qr.href.nz/)**, an open, programmable QR code generation studio and developer API.

Most online QR code tools lock basic features behind paywalls, embed redirect ads, or output low-resolution bitmaps that blur when printed on flyers, posters, or business cards. **qr.href.nz** provides a developer-grade alternative:

- **Mathematical Vector Clarity**: Export infinite-resolution SVGs ready for print layout tools, alongside pixel-perfect 300+ DPI PNGs.
- **Multiple Payload Types**: Generate QR codes for website URLs, direct Wi-Fi network joining, vCard 4.0 contact cards, WhatsApp messaging, SMS, geographic coordinates, and cryptocurrency wallets.
- **Automated REST API**: Integrate dynamic QR codes directly into your transactional emails, PDF invoices, and warehouse shipping labels via simple HTTP requests to `/v1/qr`.
- **Zero Spyware or Intermediary Interstitials**: Direct QR codes contain the exact target payload you specify, with no covert analytics redirects or tracking middleware unless you choose to route through a short link.

Start generating vector codes for free at **[qr.href.nz](https://qr.href.nz/)**.
