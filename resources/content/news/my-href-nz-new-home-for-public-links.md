If you manage `href.nz`, `meinlink.at`, or `href.yt` links with an account, meet your new home: **[my.href.nz](https://my.href.nz/)**.

The public shorteners grew up. What started as a guest-first side of the network now has members with hundreds of links — and they were sharing one dashboard with family subdomains, business links, and newsletter tracking. So we split it: a small, fast dashboard holding exactly your public links, with overview stats, per-link analytics, CSV export, and QR downloads.

Nothing moves on your side. Your links, slugs, and stats are untouched — they simply render in the new place now, and the old URLs on `dash.ternis.link` point you over. One account signs into both; API keys, domains, and settings stay on `dash.ternis.link` as before.

Guests keep the same deal: paste, shorten, copy — no account, no changes. And if you've been bumping into the 50-link daily guest limit, that is a good sign it's time to [see what an account adds](https://ternis.link/pages/blog/2026-10-05-from-guest-link-to-managed-link).
