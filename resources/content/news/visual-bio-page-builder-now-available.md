We've rolled out a major update to our bio page suite: the all-new **Visual Bio Page-Builder** is now available to all verified account holders.

Instead of configuring links in disconnected forms, creators can now assemble and preview their landing pages inside an interactive smartphone canvas with instant live reordering:

- **Interactive RSVP Blocks**: Collect attendee signups directly on your bio page, display real-time headcounts, and cap attendance automatically.
- **Rich Media Embeds**: Add embedded Spotify/Apple audio players, video embeds, and interactive map locations for upcoming meetups.
- **E-Commerce & Coupons**: Feature discount codes with one-click copy and countdown timers to build urgency around drops.
- **Custom Domains & Fast Routing**: Host your bio page directly at the root of your verified custom domain or subdomain (e.g. `bio.yourbrand.com`).
- **Privacy Parity**: Get full click and visitor counts without injecting cross-site tracking cookies into your visitors' browsers.

Access the visual editor in your account dashboard under **Bio Pages → Visual Builder**.
